Meet the particles

Particles are short words that come after a word and tell you its job in the sentence. English uses word order for this. Japanese uses particles.

わたしは　パンを　たべます。
I eat bread.　·　は marks "I" as the topic, を marks "bread" as the thing eaten.

The 8 core N5 particles

がgasubject
をoobject
にnitime · to · at

Particle Power · N52
は
Lesson 1 · read as "wa"

The topic marker

"As for X…" — what the sentence is about
Key ideaは marks the topic: the thing you are talking about. Everything after は is a comment about that topic.
Topic は comment です。
わたしは 学生がくせいです。I am a student.
これは 本ほんです。This is a book.
田中たなかさんは 先生せんせいです。Mr./Ms. Tanaka is a teacher.
今日きょうは さむいです。It's cold today.
(lit. "As for today, it's cold.")
PronunciationThe particle は is written with the kana ha but always read wa. Inside normal words, は is still ha: はな (hana, flower), はい (hai, yes).
Bonus · contrastは can also compare two things:
コーヒーは 飲のみますが、お茶ちゃは 飲のみません。
I drink coffee, but I don't drink tea.

Particle Power · N54
が
Lesson 2 · read as "ga"

The subject marker

Who or what does it, has it, likes it
Key ideaが points at the subject, often as new information. If は says "as for X…", が says "X is the one."

Use が in these 3 situations

① With question words (だれ, なに, どれ) and their answers
だれが 来きましたか。Who came?
田中たなかさんが 来きました。Tanaka came. (Tanaka is the one.)
② With "like / good at / understand" words: すき, きらい, じょうず, わかる
わたしは ねこが すきです。I like cats.
母ははは りょうりが じょうずです。My mother is good at cooking.
③ With "there is": あります (things) / います (people, animals)
つくえの うえに 本ほんが あります。There is a book on the desk.
こうえんに 犬いぬが います。There is a dog in the park.
Common mistakeA question word is never followed by は.　✗ だれは…　✓ だれが…

を
Lesson 3 · read as "o"

The object marker

The thing that receives the action
Key ideaを marks what you eat, drink, read, buy, study… It answers "do what?"
Thing を action verb
水みずを 飲のみます。I drink water.
日本語にほんごを べんきょうします。I study Japanese.
なにを 買かいましたか。What did you buy?
Watch outすき and じょうず take が, not を — they are not action verbs.　✗ ねこを すきです　✓ ねこが すきです

  1. まいあさ コーヒー 飲のみます。
  2. わたし テレビ 見みます。
  3. わたしは 犬いぬ すきです。
  1. なに たべますか。
  2. ともだち 来きました。
  3. てがみ かきました。
