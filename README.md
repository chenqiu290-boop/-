# 大唐：我的死士每天加一（暂定名）

历史穿越 · 系统流 · 纯爽文

> 别人穿越想争霸，他只想看热闹。可他的人每天都在变多，多到整个大唐都坐不住了。

## 简介

### 完整版

武德九年八月初九，李世民即位。
同一天，长安城外，程序员沈游被一条黄狗舔醒。他摘下脖子上的工牌，挂上枣树：“辞职信就不写了。”

当夜子时，到账死士一名。
次日两名，第三日三名……
人人一门随机宗师手艺：会伪造的、会口技的、会驯鹰的，还有一个专给他修脚。
绝对忠诚，但会顶嘴。

争天下？沈游摆手：五更上朝、批奏疏、挨魏征骂，不就是换个工位加班？
他只想吃喝游逛，顺便去渭水便桥边占个前排，看李二六骑退颉利。
看完戏顺手干点什么？那是后话。

麻烦在于，人每天都在多。
第二十天，二百一十。
满一年，六万六千七百九十五。

人散在糖坊、酒坊、车队、地窖，外人数不清他到底几个人。
可人头数不清，粮账数得清。

直到一夜，宫里有人把米价、车马、新挖的窖口摊满一案，算到天亮，搁下笔：
“开明坊这个沈家，今天吃的粮，怎么又比昨天多出一截？”

### 简介栏短版

武德九年八月初九，李世民登基；程序员沈游在长安城外被一条狗舔醒，外挂到账：第N天召唤N个死士，人人一门宗师手艺，忠心，但会顶嘴。他不当皇帝，只想吃喝游逛看热闹。可人每天都在多：第二十天二百一十，满一年六万六千七百九十五。长安的粮账，开始对不上了。

### 宣传语

1. 别人穿越抢龙椅，他嫌龙椅五更就得打卡。
2. 第一天一个，第二十天二百一十，满一年六万多。李二：朕不怕他造反，朕怕他今天又多了几十张嘴。
3. 人头数不清，粮账算得清：长安城里，有一户人家每天都比昨天能吃。

## Blurb (English)

**Title candidates:** *Retainer +1 Daily: Emperor Li Shimin Is Getting Nervous* (recommended) / *One More Every Day: Slacking Off in Tang Dynasty Chang'an* / *Death-Sworn +1: My Tang Dynasty Army Grows Every Midnight*

### Full

Wude 9, eighth month, ninth day. 626 AD.
Li Shimin takes the throne of the Tang.

That same day, outside Chang'an, a programmer named Shen You wakes up to a yellow dog licking his face. He pulls off his work badge, hangs it on a jujube tree, and shrugs. "Guess I'll skip the resignation letter."

At midnight, his first retainer arrives.
Day two: two more. Day three: three…

Each one comes with a single random grandmaster-level skill. A forger. A ventriloquist. A falconer. And one man who exists solely to do his pedicures.
Absolutely loyal. Absolutely willing to talk back.

Take the throne? Shen You waves it off. A 5 a.m. standup every day, a mountain of paperwork, and Wei Zheng chewing you out in front of the whole court? That's just a worse office job with longer hours.
He'd rather eat, wander, and grab a front-row seat at the Wei River bridge to watch Li Shimin ride out with six horsemen and stare down Jieli Khan.
And after the show? That's a story for later.

The problem: there are more of them every day.
Day 20: 210.
One year: 66,795.

They're scattered across sugar workshops, wineries, caravans and cellars. Nobody can count his people.
But you can count grain.

Until one night, someone in the palace spreads rice prices, cart records and freshly dug cellars across a desk, works until dawn, and finally sets down the brush:

"The Shen household in Kaiming Ward… why are they eating more today than yesterday? *Again?*"

### Short

Wude 9 (626 AD), eighth month, ninth day: Li Shimin takes the throne. Outside Chang'an, programmer Shen You wakes up to a dog licking his face, and his cheat comes online.

On Day N at midnight, N sworn retainers appear. Each has one grandmaster skill. They're loyal to the bone and very willing to talk back.

He doesn't want the throne. He wants good food, long walks and front-row seats to history.

But the numbers keep climbing. Day 20: 210. One year: 66,795.

And Chang'an's grain ledgers have stopped adding up.

### Taglines

1. Other transmigrators fight for the throne. He saw the 5 a.m. clock-in and said, "Hard pass."
2. Day 1: one. Day 20: 210. Day 365: 66,795. Li Shimin isn't worried about a rebellion. He's worried about the grocery bill.
3. You can't count his people. But you can count what they eat, and it's more every single day.
